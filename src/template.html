Every course in one place: sessions to attend, deadlines from Canvas, and the material buried in the Canvas modules, each with a link. Pick the study period and your courses at the top. Day shows one day with what is due soon, All deadlines is the flat list.

Tick boxes are yours alone. On claude.ai they follow your account, otherwise they stay in this browser. Green counts deadlines and tasks, blue counts material. The ⊘ button mutes an item that is not for you: it stays visible, stops pulsing and leaves the counts.

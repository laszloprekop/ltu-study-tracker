# Z7005E Programvaruteknik: places where the course material disagrees

Draft, 2026-10-03. Found by reading every page, assignment and linked file in the Canvas course
(https://ltuedu.instructure.com/courses/613). Only course material, nothing about any student.

---

Hi,

While planning our study weeks we read through the whole course in Canvas and found some places
where two sources give different information. We thought a list might help. Canvas item names are
quoted as they appear in the course.

**Dates and hand-ins**

1. **Hemtentamen deadline.** The assignment and the Schema say 25 Nov 17:00. The linked
   `HomeExam2025.pdf` (https://ltuedu.instructure.com/courses/613/files/38058) says "Deadline:
   December 8, 09:00" and calls it "the home reexam". An older copy in Files says December 9.
2. **Hemtentamen hand-in channel.** The PDF says the hand-in may be e-mailed as a link; the
   assignment in Canvas is a file upload.
3. **Laboration 5.** The text says "deadline 24 September, 17:00" and to upload "to the Lab3 hand-in
   area". The assignment and the Schema say 21 Oct.

**Workshops**

4. **Numbering.** Workshop 2 (3.3, 22 Oct) comes after Workshops 3 and 4 (30 Sep). The Workshop 5
   page (4.2.10) starts with "Workshop 2 instructions" and links pages named `workshop-2-group-N`,
   while the item called Workshop 2 is the SusAF one.
5. **Workshop 2 length.** Its page plans about 3 hours; the Schema gives it 10:30 to 12:00.
6. **Zoom room.** The Schema puts Workshops 1 and 5 in the course room (`j/3024506121`); their own
   pages point to a personal room.
7. **Number of groups.** Canvas has 8 project groups and the Workshop 1/5 pages cover 8, but the
   "Miro boards" page and the SusAF page list 9 boards.

**Order of preparation**

8. **Videos needed before they are scheduled.** 3.4 to 3.6 have to be watched before Workshop 4
   (30 Sep) but are allocated to week 42; 2.3 is the same.
9. **Laboration 3 builds on Laboration 4's choice.** Lab 3 (8 Oct) analyses the GitHub project
   chosen "for Laboration 4" (15 Oct). A line saying the project must be chosen before Lab 3 would
   help.
10. **Laboration 1 appendix** says to skip 2.6 Testbarhet, while the step list still asks for a test
    plan in 2.6.
11. **2.4** says "det räcker med en uppladdning per person", but there is nothing to upload to.

**Start page**

12. The start page reads like a template from another course: it mentions "three online sessions"
    (the Schema has 24), modules with View/Contribute/Submit requirements (none are set), and a
    certificate on completion.

Thank you for a good course so far.

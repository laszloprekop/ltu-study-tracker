# Z0025E Computer Networks: places where the course material disagrees

Draft, 2026-10-03. Found by reading every page, assignment, announcement and linked file in the
Canvas course (https://ltuedu.instructure.com/courses/614) and the Lab Signup Sheet. Only course
material, nothing about any student.

---

Hi,

While planning our study weeks we read through the whole course in Canvas and found some places
where two sources give different information. We thought a list might help.

**Labs**

1. **Lab 3 session time.** The Lab Signup Sheet heading says "Oct 16th - 13:00am", the slots run
   09:00 to 10:45, and the Homepage says 13:00 to 15:00. Which is right?
2. **Report deadline.** 9.1 says to submit the report "before your scheduled lab assessment"; the
   assignments are due at 23:59 the same day.
3. **Where to sign up.** The Week 3 announcement says to sign up "in Canvas"; the sign-up is the
   Google Doc (9.2 Lab Signup Sheet).
4. **Group or alone.** All six lab assignments say they can be done individually; the Assessments
   page and 9.1 say groups of 3 to 4.
5. **Lab 3 "Lab Files".** The list links copies that are locked (no download), while the inline links
   in the same section work.
6. **Lab 2 sheet** has a ninth slot at 15:00, after the session ends.

**Dates and schedule**

7. **Course introduction slides.** `L00 Course Introduction.pdf` (1.2), linked as the current
   introduction, shows the 2025 schedule: Midterm 1 on 14 Nov, Midterm 2 on 12 Dec, a final deadline
   of 22 Dec and a combined 150-point threshold. Canvas says 23 Oct, 20 Nov, quizzes by 2 Dec and 75
   points per midterm.
8. **"Check the Canvas calendar."** The Homepage and 9.1 ask students to subscribe to the course
   calendar, but it has no events. The Homepage table is the only calendar.
9. **Midterm location.** The Homepage says "Zoom"; the midterms are Canvas quizzes open for 24 hours.
10. **Midterm Overview** lists Midterm 2's coverage as "Module 5.8".

**Structure**

11. **Two items numbered 3.4** (the Lecture 7 discussion and the Module 3 Quiz). Module 2 lists 2.4
    before 2.3 and has no 2.2.
12. **5.2** is a page with a "contribute" requirement, which cannot be met on a page.
13. **0.1** mentions one poll per module for eight modules; there are seven.
14. **Support links.** 18 pages and discussions point to a forum in course 23566 on
    `canvas.ltu.se`.
15. **Recordings.** The recordings on 2.4 and 3.2 come from course 453. Are they this year's?

Thank you for a good course so far.

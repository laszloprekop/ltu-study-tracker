## Entity kinds

### 1. Scheduled session (lecture, workshop, seminar)
- **Where:** Schema page (`/courses/613/pages/schema`), under "Schemalagda sessioner", a `<p>`
  day heading ("Tisdag 22 september") then `<li>` lines.
- **Machine-readable:** parseable prose. 22 lines.
  `^(\d{1,2}:\d{2})(?:\s*-\s*(\d{1,2}:\d{2}))?:\s*(.+)$` under day heading
  `^(Måndag|Tisdag|Onsdag|Torsdag|Fredag)\s+(\d{1,2})\s+([a-zåäö]+)$`. Example line:
  `10:30 - 11:45: Del 1.5 – Föreläsning 6 i Zoom ( https://ltu-se.zoom.us/j/3024506121 )`.
  The link text `Del X.Y` is the module item number; the link `href` is the page URL.
- **Missing end time:** 8 of 22 lines give only a start (`09:15: Del 1.4 ...`). The Schema
  states the block format: "30 min undervisning + 5 min rast + 30 min undervisning, 10 min rast,
  30 + 5 + 30, 10 min diskussion och reserv". So a 09:15 start without an end is the first half of
  a double block: 09:15 to 10:20, and the 10:30 line is the second half. Derived, not stated.
- **Year is absent** from every date ("Torsdag 17 september"). The year comes from context.
- **Examples:** 22 Sep 13:00 to 16:00 Workshop 1 (1.7); 30 Sep 10:30 to 11:45 Workshop 4
  (3.6.7); 5 Nov 09:15 to 12:00 "slutseminarier - presentationer" (no page link; 6.1 exists but
  the Schema line does not link it).
- **Multi-ref sessions:** 29 Sep 10:30 covers "Föreläsning 10 och delar av Föreläsning 9 och
  Föreläsning 12" (3.1, 2.2, 4.1). One slot, three pages, two of them partial.
- **Leads to:** recordings. Session pages are edited the day of or after the session to add the
  recording (1.1 to 1.3 updated 18 Sep, 1.4 to 1.6 on 23 Sep, 2.1 and 3.1 on 29 Sep). Pages for
  future sessions still carry a "Gammal inspelning" (old recording) from 24 Aug. A page's
  `updated_at` after its session date is a usable "recording is up" signal.

### 2. Workshop step plan (minute-level agenda inside a session)
- **Where:** Workshop 1 (1.7) and Workshop 5 (4.2.10) link 8 per-group pages each, not in any

- **Machine-readable:** parseable prose. `Step (\d+) \((\d+)(?:\+(\d+))?\s*min\)`. Example:

  Del 4 = 15 min, total cirka 3 timmar". The Schema gives it 10:30 to 12:00 (90 min). See
  Surprises.
- **Group pairing is structured:** group N is client for group N+1 and developer for group N-1,
  modulo 8 (group 1 client for 2, developer for 8). The same pairing is reused in Workshop 5.
- **Leads to:** Workshop 5 "builds upon the design created in the previous workshop" (Workshop
  1, 22 Sep, to Workshop 5, 23 Oct). The Workshop 1 Miro boards are reused in Workshop 5
  (identical `uXjVLX...` board links).

### 3. Hand-in deadline (assignment)

- **Machine-readable:** structured (`due_at`). Prose patterns, if needed:
  `^Vecka (\d+) (\d{1,2})/(\d{1,2}) \((\d{1,2}:\d{2})\) - \[(Grupp|Individuell)\]` and

## The chain to the weighted events

```

21 Sep  start choosing GitHub project ─► Lab 3, 8 Oct (patterns IN that project)
                                     └► Lab 4, 15 Oct (architecture; "consider assignment 6")

5 Nov   09:00 Lab 7 slides uploaded ─► 09:15 seminar presentation ◄──┘
Workshop 6 (6 Nov, "hemtenta 2015" refactoring) + 5.6 example exam + 5.1 unit testing
        + watch 5.3, 5.4 ─► home exam, 25 Nov 17:00 (individual)
                               └► book oral (link after 25 Nov) ─► oral, week 49, ~15 min
```

## Surprises and contradictions

1. **Exam deadline:** Canvas and the Schema say 25 Nov 17:00. The linked PDF (file 38058,
   `HomeExam2025.pdf`, uploaded 17 Sep) says "Deadline – December 8, 09:00" and "The home reexam
   is an individual examination". It is last year's re-exam text; Canvas wins, but a student
   reading the PDF sees another date. An unlinked older copy (38080) says December 9.
2. **Exam submission channel:** the PDF says the hand-in may be e-mailed as a link; the
   assignment is `online_upload` in Canvas.
3. **Lab 5 header says "deadline 24 September, 17:00"**; `due_at` and the Schema say 21 Oct.
   Lab 5 also says to upload "to the Lab3 hand-in area" (old numbering).
4. **Interview length:** Lab 1 says 30 minutes shared by 3 groups. `plan.mjs` says "45 minutes,
   shared with two other groups". The 30 is from Canvas.
5. **Workshop numbering is not chronological** and one page is mislabelled: Workshop 2 (3.3, 22
   Oct) runs after Workshops 3 and 4 (30 Sep). Workshop 5's page (4.2.10) starts with "Workshop 2
   instructions" and links pages named `workshop-2-group-N`, while the Canvas item called Workshop
   2 is the SusAF one.
6. **Workshop 2 time:** its page plans about 3 hours; the Schema gives it 90 minutes (10:30 to
   12:00). Either it is cut short or the rest is group work after the session.
7. **Group counts:** Canvas has 8 project groups; Workshop 1/5 pages cover 8; the Home "Miro
   boards" page and the SusAF page list 9 boards; the SusAF text says "grupp 1 - 8".
8. **Two Zoom rooms:** the Schema puts every session, Workshops 1 and 5 included, in
   `j/3024506121`; the Workshop 1 and 5 pages say to go to `my/josef.hallberg`.
9. **The start page is a template from another course:** "three online sessions" (there are 24
   scheduled lines), "modules are set with prerequisites ... requirement View/Contribute/Submit"
   (all 8 modules have none, 0 completion requirements), and a certificate on completion. Module 0
   discussions speak of "Week 1 / Week 2" of a separate MOOC; those weeks have no dates.
10. **Self-study vs prep:** 3.4 to 3.6 must be watched before 30 Sep (Workshop 4) but are
    allocated to week 42; 2.3 the same. Already noted in `plan.mjs`, still true in Canvas.
11. **Lab 3 depends on a later lab's choice:** Lab 3 (8 Oct) analyses the GitHub project chosen
    "for Laboration 4" (15 Oct). The choice is the real precondition, prompted only by the 21 Sep
    to-do line.
12. **2.4 says "det räcker med en uppladdning per person"** but there is no assignment to upload
    to. Optional and ungraded, but it reads like a hand-in.
13. **Lab 1 appendix** says to skip 2.6 Testbarhet and chapter 4, while the step list still asks
    for a test plan in 2.6.
14. **All assignments were edited on 30 Sep** (`updated_at`), dates unchanged against the
    inventory. The front page (`/front_page`) is a Module 0 wellbeing page, while the course opens
    on Modules (`default_view: modules`), so "front page" is not the start page.
15. **"Schema" is not linked from the start page by that name:** the start page calls it
    "Schedule"; the Schema itself was last edited 17 Sep, so changes after that (if any) reach
    students only by other channels. With no announcements in this course, a moved session would
    show only as an edit to this page: watch `updated_at` of `pages/schema`.

## Privacy note

counts are aggregate. The token owner's own group membership (`/users/self/groups`) was read
but is not recorded. Only the teacher's name, already public on the course pages, appears.
